Dwarven Descent

Descend through the mine, gather ore, gold, and crystals, and reach the exit at the bottom. Use cave-ins, supports, and unstable terrain to outsmart enemies instead of fighting fair.

Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys move, attack, or climb 1-high blocks. Hold Shift while moving to mine. Space waits, Tab expands the log, ? or Esc toggles help, and R restarts.

Gamepad: left stick or d-pad moves. Button 0 modifies movement to mine, button 1 waits, button 2 expands the log, and Start toggles help or restarts after a run ends.

The emergency grapple catches overhead stone within 5 tiles.

Press Esc or Start button to restart.
